About Us

A youth-led community program bringing affordable, coach-led badminton to Vaughan.

How Glen Shields Started

For years, accessing badminton in Vaughan felt out of reach. The closest coach-led programs were over ten kilometres away and cost more than forty dollars per session. For many families in the community, this created a real barrier, young people who wanted to play simply could not afford the time or cost to participate.

Glen Shields Badminton was founded by youth, for youth. We came together with a shared goal: make badminton affordable, local, and welcoming. Today we are one of the few coach-led badminton programs in Vaughan, operating in the east side of the city where youth programs, especially badminton, are scarce.

We run organized sessions out of rented school gymnasium courts, keeping costs low so that more families can participate. Every week, participants build fitness, make friends, and find a community they can count on.

What to Expect

Structured Coaching

Each session includes warm-ups, technique drills, and open play, led by experienced coaches who give clear, constructive feedback.

All Skill Levels Welcome

From complete beginners to more experienced players, our coaches adjust instruction to meet each participant where they are.

Consistent Location Each Year

Each year is held at a single school gymnasium. Your location is confirmed at registration and stays the same for all sessions that year.

Affordable, Flexible Pricing

Drop in for $25 per session, or commit to a term of 9 classes for $180. No long contracts, no hidden fees.
